Why it matters

Fitness isn't one thing. That's the point.

Some people are here to build strength. Some are managing stress, recovering from injury, or trying to keep up with their kids. The research is consistent even when the reasons aren't — regular movement is tied to better sleep, mood, heart health, and long-term independence.
