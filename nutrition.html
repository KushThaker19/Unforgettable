Nutrition

Food that supports the work, not another rulebook.

No crash diets, no forbidden foods. Just the basics that actually move the needle, explained plainly. Beginner. Intermediate. Advanced. Doesn't matter!

Protein

Aim for enough, spread out throughout your day

Most people doing regular strength work benefit from protein at every meal — think eggs, beans, poultry, fish, tofu, or dairy — rather than saving it all for dinner. People think there aren't vegetarian or vegan friendly options, but there are plenty!

Carbs

Fuel, not the enemy

Carbohydrates are the body's preferred energy source for exercise. More Carbs = More ATP. It's going to give you that "Oomf." Whole grains, fruit, and starchy vegetables support harder training sessions and recovery. These are perfect "fast digesting carbs" to intake before a workout.

Water

The easiest win

Hydrate! Even mild dehydration can affect strength and endurance. A simple habit: drink a glass of water when you wake up and another before each workout. Eventually, increase the amount and what seemed like an annoying task will later become a natural part of your day.

Building a plate

A simple way to think about meals

You don't need to weigh every gram. A useful mental model many dietitians recommend:

  • Fill half the plate with vegetables or fruit
  • A quarter with a protein source
  • A quarter with whole grains or starchy vegetables
  • A thumb-sized portion of healthy fats (olive oil, nuts, avocado)
  • Remember to enjoy the food you eat! Don't make it seem like a chore.
  • Get creative with your meals because you might find out that you actually enjoy them!
